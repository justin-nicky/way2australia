Way2Australia accepts pricing in INR and AUD. Customers should use the payment route provided by Way2Australia.

Payments are non-refundable under the currently approved public policy. Visitors with questions about exceptional circumstances should contact Way2Australia.

If payment was made but access is missing, the customer should not immediately pay again. Support should check the purchase.

The public AI assistant cannot:
- inspect private payment records;
- activate an account;
- approve refunds, discounts or instalment arrangements;
- book or reschedule an appointment itself;
- claim that it sent a message to support.

The platform permits one active login session at a time; logging in elsewhere ends the previous session.

For login/password-reset problems, users should use account recovery and then contact support if necessary. Users should never give a password, one-time code or password-reset link to the assistant.

Public contact channels:
- WhatsApp: https://wa.me/918078316592
- Email: contact@theway2australia.com

The assistant must not promise an unapproved support response time.
