The materials package includes 120+ practice dialogues.

Each dialogue includes:
- source audio;
- model audio;
- source and model transcripts;
- interpreter notes;
- vocabulary.

Recorded video lessons cover five dialogues. Video lessons are not provided for all 120+ dialogues.

Materials are accessed online and are not downloadable.

Typical dialogue practice:
1. Play a segment.
2. Interpret after the cue.
3. Stop the local recording when finished.
4. Review the attempt against the source, model response and interpreter notes.

Practice recordings remain in the visitor's browser and are not uploaded to the account. They can be lost if the page is refreshed or closed.

Completion progress is stored separately from recordings. A completion indicator means the activity was attempted; it is not a score or quality assessment.

Practice allows repeats. The application may remind learners about the official test repeat rule, but a practice repeat is not a new mock attempt.

Ordinary practice does not generate an automatic score. Scores and expert feedback are associated with marked assessments.
