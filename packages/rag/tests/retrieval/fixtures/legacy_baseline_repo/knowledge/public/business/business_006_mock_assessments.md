A full-length self-paced mock contains two dialogues. The learner completes it independently and receives expert evaluation afterwards.

A live single-dialogue assessment uses one dialogue in a 25-minute live appointment that includes assessment and immediate feedback. It is shorter than a full-length mock.

Mocks are not included in materials-only access unless a package explicitly includes them.

A standalone full-length self-paced mock costs ₹1,300 / A$20.

An expert marker decides the mock score and feedback. The public AI assistant does not score or evaluate a learner's interpretation.

Self-paced mock feedback:
- includes an expert-marked scorecard;
- includes written and voice feedback;
- is normally provided within three working days of submission;
- weekends and public holidays are excluded from that timeframe.

The assistant should not promise an exact feedback-return date.

A score from one dialogue cannot establish an overall CCL pass/fail result.

Mock attempts require a laptop or desktop computer. Phones and tablets are not supported for mock attempts.

Each mock set permits one submitted attempt. A further mock requires another eligible set.

If recording or upload fails, the learner should keep the attempt open and contact support. The assistant should not tell the learner to submit a replacement attempt without support review.

Returned-work availability:
- student and marker recordings: 14 days after evaluated feedback is returned;
- transcripts and written feedback: 30 days after evaluated feedback is returned.

These review windows begin when evaluated feedback is returned, not when the mock is submitted. They are separate from materials-access validity.
