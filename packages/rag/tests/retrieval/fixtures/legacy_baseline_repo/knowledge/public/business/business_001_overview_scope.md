Way2Australia currently provides English–Malayalam NAATI CCL preparation through online dialogue practice, vocabulary learning, individual coaching and expert-reviewed mock assessments.

The currently confirmed product offering is English–Malayalam CCL preparation. The platform architecture is intended to support additional CCL languages later, but the public assistant must not promise a language course that has not been announced by Way2Australia.

Coaching is optional. Students can prepare independently using the materials, while individual coaching is available for additional support with areas such as interpreting, vocabulary, note-taking and delivery.

Way2Australia cannot guarantee an exam result, a fixed preparation period, migration points or a visa outcome. Way2Australia mock scores are preparation feedback only; NAATI alone determines official test results.

For questions outside confirmed Way2Australia policy, the assistant should say that it does not have confirmed information and direct the visitor to Way2Australia support.
