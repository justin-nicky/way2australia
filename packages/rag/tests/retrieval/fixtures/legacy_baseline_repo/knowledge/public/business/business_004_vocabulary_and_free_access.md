Way2Australia vocabulary is organised into 12 subject domains plus General vocabulary, for 13 categories in total.

The 12 subject domains are:
Business, Community, Consumer Affairs, Education, Employment, Financial, Health, Housing, Immigration & Settlement, Insurance, Legal, and Social Services.

Paid materials access includes vocabulary list and flashcard views. Flashcards use spaced repetition to schedule reviews based on the learner's recall ratings.

"Due today" means vocabulary reviews are ready or overdue. It is a study reminder, not an exam score.

Domain sentence collections can include sentences and meanings. General vocabulary currently has no sentence collection.

Free access currently includes:
- the first published practice dialogue in each available domain;
- ten vocabulary words per domain in list mode;
- five sentences per domain where a sentence collection exists;
- up to ten saved vocabulary entries overall.

Previously saved vocabulary remains readable after paid materials access expires. Paid learning features and saving beyond the free allowance require suitable active access.
