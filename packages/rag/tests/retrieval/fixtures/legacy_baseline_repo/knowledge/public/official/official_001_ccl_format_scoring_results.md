The NAATI Credentialed Community Language (CCL) test assesses the ability to interpret community conversations between English and another language.

Current official format:
- two pre-recorded dialogues;
- each dialogue is worth 45 marks;
- total available marks: 90;
- pass requirement: at least 63/90 overall and at least 29/45 in each dialogue.

A candidate can therefore fail even with an overall score of 63 or more if one dialogue is below 29.

NAATI uses a deduction-based assessment approach. Important areas include accuracy, language quality/register and delivery.

Candidates must begin interpreting within five seconds of the chime. Excessive pauses, repeated corrections and excessive segment repeats can result in deductions.

Official CCL results are normally sent by email within 4–6 weeks.

CCL is not professional interpreter or translator certification.
