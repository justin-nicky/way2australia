For Malayalam CCL, NAATI recognises regional variations and dialects. A candidate is not penalised merely for using a Kerala regional dialect as long as the language would be understood by an average Malayalee living in Kerala.

Malayalam has adopted words from several languages, including English. Limited use of English words may be acceptable when the word is conventionally used and widely understood, particularly in scientific, medical and technical contexts or where an accurate Malayalam translation is unavailable.

Where a commonly used Malayalam equivalent exists, NAATI prefers the Malayalam term.

For numbers, NAATI's Malayalam policy expects numbers to be rendered in Malayalam rather than simply repeated in English/Hindi numerals. Figures expressed in millions should be converted appropriately to lakhs/crores and vice versa.

This is a Malayalam-specific policy. As additional CCL languages are introduced into Way2Australia, each language should have its own official language-policy document rather than applying Malayalam rules globally.
