The following learning material is approved for public chatbot use.

Sample dialogue:
English source:
"Hi there! I'm Tom. I noticed you've just moved in—welcome to the neighbourhood!"

Malayalam model:
"നമസ്കാരം! ഞാൻ ടോം. നിങ്ങൾ ഇവിടെ പുതിയതായി താമസം മാറിയതാണെന്ന് ഞാൻ ശ്രദ്ധിച്ചു - നമ്മുടെ അയൽപക്കത്തേക്ക് സ്വാഗതം!"

Interpreter note:
This is a warm, casual neighbour greeting. The Malayalam should remain friendly and light rather than formal or official.

Public vocabulary sample:
- offers → വാഗ്ദാനങ്ങൾ, in a housing context.

Only explicitly approved public samples should be exposed by the public assistant. The full paid dialogue library, full vocabulary library, hidden answers and mock-pool content must not be copied into the public chatbot corpus.
