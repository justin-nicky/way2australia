## Official NAATI CCL test information

These answers describe the official test, not Way2Australia’s practice settings.

**62. What is the CCL test?**

It assesses interpreting community conversations between English and another language.

**63. How many dialogues are in the official test?**

Two recorded dialogues, divided into segments.

**64. What is the pass mark?**

At least 63/90 overall, with at least 29/45 in each dialogue.

**65. If I get 63 overall but only 28 in one dialogue, have I passed?**

No. Both requirements must be met.

**66. Is a coaching course compulsory?**

No. NAATI does not require a preparation course.

**67. Does passing CCL certify me to work as an interpreter or translator?**

No. CCL is not professional interpreter or translator certification.

Source: [NAATI CCL information](https://www.naati.com.au/migration-assessments/ccl/).

**68. How quickly must I begin interpreting?**

Within five seconds of the chime.

**69. Can I repeat a segment?**

One repeated segment per dialogue is allowed without penalty. Additional repeats attract deductions.

**70. Can I correct myself?**

Yes. Signal the correction; excessive corrections and pauses can affect delivery.

**71. Can I take notes?**

Use a pen and loose paper. Prepared notes, typed notes and electronic resources are prohibited.

**72. Should I interpret as “I” or say “he/she said”?**

Use direct speech/first person rather than reporting the conversation indirectly.

**73. Can I use headphones?**

No headphones, headsets or earphones are allowed during the official test.

**74. Can I take the official test using only my phone?**

No. A computer is required, with a separate phone/tablet used for proctoring.

**75. How long is the test?**

The performance recording must be under 20 minutes, including source audio and responses. Setup and navigation time are separate.

Source: [Official candidate instructions and equipment requirements](https://www.naati.com.au/resources/candidate-instructions-ccl/).

**76. Is my regional Malayalam dialect acceptable?**

NAATI allows regional variation when it would be understood by an average Malayalee living in Kerala.

**77. Can I use English words while interpreting into Malayalam?**

Limited conventional borrowing is allowed. NAATI prefers a commonly used Malayalam equivalent where one exists. This is not blanket permission to leave unfamiliar words untranslated.

**78. Can I say numbers in English while interpreting into Malayalam?**

NAATI’s Malayalam policy requires Malayalam numbers and appropriate conversion between millions and lakhs/crores.

Source: [NAATI’s Malayalam language policy](https://www.naati.com.au/resources/language-policy-ccl/).

## Official booking results and validity

**83. How do I book the official CCL test?**

Apply through myNAATI. After approval, select an available date and pay.

**84. How late can I book?**

Current guidance says bookings close one week before the test.

**85. Can I reschedule the official test?**

Current CCL guidance allows free rescheduling until eight days before the test, through myNAATI.

**86. Can I cancel the official test and receive a refund?**

Current CCL guidance provides a 75% refund for cancellation at least one hour before the scheduled start, subject to its terms.

These are NAATI’s policies, separate from Way2Australia’s policies. Source: [Official booking and cancellation guidance](https://www.naati.com.au/migration-assessments/ccl/).

**87. What is the official test fee?**

The published fee is currently A$814. Confirm the applicable amount in myNAATI before paying.

**88. What does an official result review cost?**

NAATI currently lists A$187, subject to review eligibility.

Source: [NAATI’s 2026–27 fee schedule](https://www.naati.com.au/resources/fees/).

**89. Where can I check available test dates?**

Use the [official NAATI date finder](https://www.naati.com.au/test-date/), then confirm availability in myNAATI.

**90. Which time zone is used?**

NAATI lists test times in Sydney/Canberra time. Convert using your actual test date because daylight saving changes the offset. [Candidate instructions](https://www.naati.com.au/resources/candidate-instructions-ccl/).

**91. Can I take CCL from India or another country?**

Yes. CCL is conducted online, subject to meeting the test requirements.

**92. Can someone guarantee an earlier test date?**

NAATI states that companies and individuals do not receive preferential access to test places.

**93. How long is a CCL result valid?**

Results issued from 9 August 2022 have five-year validity from issue. Earlier results had three years.

**94. Can I extend an expired CCL credential?**

No. NAATI requires a new test rather than extending the credential’s expiry.

Source: [NAATI FAQs](https://www.naati.com.au/resources/faq/).

**95. When will official results arrive?**

NAATI states four to six weeks. This is separate from Way2Australia’s mock-feedback timeframe.

**96. What if a technical problem occurs during my official test?**

Contact NAATI support and report the incident within 24 hours. Do not assume a refund or replacement is guaranteed.

**97. Can I request disability-related test adjustments?**

Contact NAATI early through its reasonable-adjustment process. Way2Australia’s assistant cannot approve accommodations.

Source: [NAATI terms and support procedures](https://www.naati.com.au/resources/terms-and-conditions/).

**98. Will passing guarantee my migration points or visa approval?**

The assistant cannot determine personal migration eligibility or guarantee a visa outcome. Confirm your circumstances with Home Affairs or a qualified migration adviser. [NAATI’s migration guidance](https://www.naati.com.au/migration-assessments/ccl/).
