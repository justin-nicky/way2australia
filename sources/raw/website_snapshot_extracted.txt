The person behind your preparation
Hello, I'm Niveditha.
I began my CCL journey in 2021, when Malayalam was first included in the NAATI CCL test. As there were no preparation materials for the language at the time, I started by developing some of the first Malayalam CCL resources, and later began tutoring. Since then, I have been fortunate to work with over 1,000 students as they prepared for this exam.
Each dialogue here has been written and curated with care, and the curriculum has grown from what I have learned working alongside my students, especially the areas they found most challenging. I hope the materials, practice, and feedback here make your preparation a little easier.
Whether you are just starting out or preparing for another attempt, thank you for being here. It would be a privilege to be a small part of your journey.
Practise well, and all the very best.
Niveditha

What is the NAATI CCL test, and how is it scored?
The NAATI CCL test assesses your ability to interpret everyday conversations between English and Malayalam. You interpret two dialogues and need at least
63
/
90
overall, with
29
/
45
in each dialogue. Passing can support a claim for five Australian skilled-migration points, subject to your visa eligibility.

.
How does the platform work?
Start with the free text sample to explore our approach. Choose your materials, practise dialogues and vocabulary, then use mock feedback to guide your next steps. The online library and mock platform are being prepared; contact us for current preparation options.
Try a sample segment
.
Do I need one-to-one coaching?
No. You can prepare independently with the materials, regular dialogue practice and mock feedback. One-to-one sessions are optional, for extra support with the areas you find difficult.
Are one-to-one sessions flexible?
Yes. Session times are arranged around your convenience, subject to availability. If something changes, contact your coach to discuss rearranging the session.
How are mock exams marked?
An expert marker decides your score and feedback, never AI. For a self-paced mock, you receive a scorecard with segment-level feedback within 72 hours of submission. Live one-to-one mocks include immediate feedback and suggestions from your trainer.
How can I pay?
You can choose Indian rupees, including UPI, or Australian dollars. Use the currency switch above to compare prices, and contact us to arrange your chosen option. Online checkout is not open yet.
