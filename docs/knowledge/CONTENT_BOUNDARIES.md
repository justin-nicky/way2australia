# Public assistant content boundaries

Include:
- approved public packages and policies;
- public learning samples;
- approved public FAQ answers;
- current official NAATI rules;
- current official migration-points information;
- approved contact/help routes.

Exclude:
- full paid dialogue/vocabulary libraries;
- mock-pool content and hidden answers;
- student recordings, scores and feedback;
- payment records;
- credentials and private links;
- internal project/admin information;
- unannounced product offerings;
- invented success rates or guarantees.

The public assistant is informational. It cannot perform private account actions or make business-policy exceptions.
