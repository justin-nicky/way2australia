Approved public packages and services:

- Practice materials: ₹5,500 / A$81. Includes 60-day online materials access.
- Full-length self-paced mock: ₹1,300 / A$20. Includes two dialogues and expert-marked feedback afterwards.
- Individual coaching session: ₹1,999 / A$30. One live 60-minute coaching session.
- Materials + personal guidance: ₹7,999 / A$119. Includes materials, one 60-minute coaching session and one full-length self-paced mock.
- Materials + three mocks: ₹8,499 / A$129. Includes materials and three full-length self-paced mocks.
- Personal coaching package: ₹16,000 / A$236. Includes materials, six 60-minute coaching sessions, three additional 25-minute live single-dialogue assessments, and two full-length self-paced mocks.

Validity:
- Package validity is 60 days from payment.
- Included sessions and mocks must be used within the validity period.
- Extension, pause, upgrade-credit, discount and instalment questions must be referred to Way2Australia; the assistant must not make commitments that are not explicitly approved.
