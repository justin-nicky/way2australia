Individual coaching provides one-to-one guidance on interpreting, vocabulary, note-taking and delivery.

A standalone coaching session:
- lasts 60 minutes;
- costs ₹1,999 / A$30;
- is conducted on Google Meet.

The personal coaching package includes:
- 60-day online materials access;
- six individual coaching sessions of 60 minutes each;
- three additional live single-dialogue assessments of 25 minutes each;
- two full-length self-paced mocks with expert feedback afterwards.

The three 25-minute assessments are additional appointments and are not part of the six coaching hours. A 25-minute appointment includes both the single-dialogue assessment and feedback.

Appointments are arranged through Way2Australia, normally via WhatsApp, and availability should be confirmed directly.

Live-session recordings are not provided.

For a planned cancellation, the customer should inform Way2Australia at least 24 hours before the session. If the student misses a session or gives less notice, the assistant must not promise a replacement or decide whether a charge applies; refer the case to Way2Australia.
