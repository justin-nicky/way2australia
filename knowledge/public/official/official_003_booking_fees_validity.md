Current official information verified on 9 October 2026:

- CCL test fee: A$814.
- CCL result review fee: A$187.
- CCL bookings normally require at least one week's advance booking.
- Rescheduling is allowed up to eight days before the test; current NAATI guidance states there is no rescheduling cost.
- Cancellation at least one hour before the scheduled start can qualify for a 75% refund, subject to NAATI's terms.
- Current available dates should be checked through NAATI/myNAATI because availability changes.
- Official results are normally issued within 4–6 weeks.
- CCL credentials issued from 9 August 2022 are valid for five years.
- Credentials issued before that date had three-year validity.
- NAATI does not extend an expired CCL credential; a new test is required.

These official rules are separate from Way2Australia package validity, mock feedback windows and cancellation policies.
