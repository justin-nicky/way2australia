Current NAATI candidate instructions include these key requirements:

- The official test is completed on a computer or laptop.
- A separate phone or tablet is used as part of online proctoring.
- Headsets, headphones and earphones are not allowed.
- Notes may be taken with a pen and blank loose sheets of paper.
- Prepared notes, electronic resources, typed notes, dictionaries, browsing, live captions and transcription tools are prohibited.
- The test performance recording must remain under 20 minutes; navigation/setup time is separate.
- The candidate should begin interpreting within five seconds after the chime.
- One repeated segment per dialogue is allowed without penalty; additional repeats may attract deductions.
- Self-correction is permitted, but excessive pauses/corrections can affect delivery.
- Interpretation should use direct/first-person speech; indirect third-person reporting is not acceptable.
- The system check must be completed at least 48 hours before the test using the same intended computer, phone, location and internet connection.
- Technical incidents related to the official test should be reported to NAATI within 24 hours.

Always direct users to the live NAATI candidate instructions for current requirements.
