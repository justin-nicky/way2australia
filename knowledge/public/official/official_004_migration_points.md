The Australian Department of Home Affairs points tables currently award 5 points for holding a recognised qualification in a credentialled community language when the relevant points-test requirements are met.

The public assistant must not infer that a person is automatically eligible for a visa or guaranteed migration points merely because they pass CCL. Individual eligibility depends on the applicable visa rules and the person's circumstances.

For personal migration eligibility, direct the visitor to current Home Affairs information or an appropriately qualified migration adviser.
